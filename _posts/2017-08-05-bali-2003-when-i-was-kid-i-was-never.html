-- Bali 2003 --
When I was a kid, I never thought that I will live this far from home. All the things that happened to me was just flowing like a wind. So fast and here I am, 24 years old in the middle of Jakarta City, it's 700 miles from home.

Despite all of that, I'm so lucky being surrounded by good and great partners, they made my journey completed, I don't even ask myself to meet people like them, they just come and we're partner. But one thing I used to believe, God is always with them.
