when I first saw You
thought You were nothing
month have passing and now
You mean everything

I scare to love You
but my heart told me to...

I'm shy to tell You 
how I feel cause
I might lose You
and that what I fear

You the only one that
make me feel this way and now
can't stop thinking about You

I can't find the word 
to tell You how I feel
all I can say that I love You
please don't tears my heart into two
I don't want to love another but 
YOU :')
